---

Read `docs/UX.md` and `docs/PRODUCT_BRIEF.md` when touching FACC UI.

- Prioritize a fast answer to "Cosa facciamo oggi/nel weekend vicino a noi?" over vanity numbers.
- Public listing and details require no sign-in. Manual locality and time are first-class; device location/map only on request.
- Show real practical constraints: event time, place, price state, ages, booking, organizer, last verification and source. Distinguish unknown from free/safe/accessible.
- Keep the listing usable without maps, geolocation permissions, large imagery or JavaScript-only interactions when feasible.
- Make every synthetic item prominently identifiable as DEMO. No invented social proof, ratings or buy tickets action.
- Forms for organizers must be progressive and understandable, with clear dates when duplicating events and visible draft/cancellation states.
- Verify at 320px, touch and keyboard; semantic headings/forms, contrast, accessible focus, meaningful empty/loading/error states and correct Italian microcopy.
- Prefer a distinctive local editorial visual direction, not generic marketing blocks or needless dashboards; respect actual brand guidelines when provided.
- When showing a UI result, state precisely what you inspected visually and which viewport or evidence is missing.
